Where I’ve worked

From SQL and PL/SQL as an intern to backend services for enterprise logistics — a short history, in two parts.

Part one 2023 — present

2023–

Shipsy

Backend Developer · started as an intern

Shipsy is a leading logistics software solution provider, and since 2023 I’ve worked there as a Backend Developer — first as an intern, then full time. The job: building and maintaining scalable backend services for enterprise transportation and supply chain management platforms.

Day to day that means designing and building microservices and RESTful APIs, and the high-throughput data pipelines behind them. The work runs across database optimisation, distributed systems and cloud infrastructure — the unglamorous plumbing that has to hold up under load when shipments are moving.

It’s also where drawing quietly helps: an API that feels obvious, or a schema that feels right, comes from the same instinct for structure and for knowing what to leave out.
