Plate 01 E-Commerce · C2C

Veggy

An e-commerce application to market agricultural products — straight from the farmer to the consumer.

View on GitHub
Type
E-Commerce · Consumer to Consumer
For
Farmers and local consumers
Source
github.com/DVamsee/veggy

The website guides the consumer to compare the quality and price of a product against the market rate, and builds a platform for consumers to check the availability of a product in their surrounding areas. It acts as a unique and secure way to perform agro-marketing.

E-farming serves as a way for farmers to sell their products for a better price with only basic knowledge of how to use the website. The project enables users to purchase products directly from the farmer — Consumer to Consumer (C2C) commerce.
