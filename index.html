p.03From the editor

Logic by day, line by night.

Hello, and welcome to the issue. By day I design and build scalable microservices, RESTful APIs and high-throughput data pipelines — currently for enterprise logistics platforms at Shipsy, where I started as an intern and now work as a backend developer across database optimisation, distributed systems and cloud infrastructure.

Off the clock, I draw: black-and-white ink and pencil, mostly superheroes, Krishna and geometric pieces. Drawing trains the eye for structure, proportion and negative space — the same instincts that make an API feel obvious or a schema feel right. Both come down to knowing what to leave out.

That’s why this issue is printed in just two inks.

— VamseeEditor, engineer, occasional illustrator
